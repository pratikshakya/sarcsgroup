Honest answers

The questions every CPA
firm actually asks us

"We tried offshore before and it didn't work."
The most common reason offshore arrangements fail is a lack of proper management. The practice ends up managing a remote hire directly — without the tools, time, or context to do it well. We are the management layer. We supervise your staff member daily, review output quality, manage performance issues, and replace if needed. You give task direction. We handle everything else.
"How do we know our clients' data is safe?"
Every staff member signs a comprehensive NDA before their first day. Your client data is accessed only through your own platform — Xero, QuickBooks, or MYOB — with individual credentials you control and can revoke instantly. We use time-tracking software with activity monitoring. We can provide our full data security protocol document on request, before you commit to anything.
"We can't afford to add another cost right now."
This is almost always a cost-reduction move, not an additional cost. A mid-level AU bookkeeper costs $62,000+ annually all-in. Our Standard placement costs approximately AUD $26,000 per year — everything included. Most practices that engage us are replacing a local hire, not adding to their headcount. The saving starts from month one.
"How do we know the quality will actually be good?"
All staff are tested on your specific software before placement. The first 30 days include intensive daily supervision — we review every output and fine-tune the workflow before stepping back. If you are not satisfied in the first 30 days you can request a replacement for any reason. We also offer a structured quality SLA in our service agreement, so expectations are in writing from day one.
"Are these employees or freelancers?"
They are SARCS Group employees — on our Nepal payroll, covered by Nepal labour law, receiving social security contributions, and entitled to leave and benefits. You are not their employer and you have zero Nepal employment legal exposure. This is fundamentally different from hiring a freelancer through Upwork or a similar platform, where the legal risk and management burden are yours.
"What if we want to stop after a few months?"
Our minimum is three months — not a year-long lock-in. That minimum exists because a bookkeeping placement takes 4–6 weeks to reach full productivity, and one month wouldn't give you a fair evaluation. After the three-month minimum, it's month-to-month with 30 days' written notice. No penalty, no drama.

What software do your bookkeepers work with?+
Our standard placement covers Xero, QuickBooks Online, and MYOB. All candidates are skills-tested on your specific platform before placement — not just self-reported. We can also accommodate Reckon, FreshBooks, and other platforms with slightly longer lead times for sourcing.
How does the time zone overlap work practically?+
Nepal is UTC+5:45. For Australian Eastern clients, this means your bookkeeper is working from approximately 3:15 AM AEST — so their full output is ready when you arrive at work. We agree a 2-hour live overlap window at the start of the engagement for real-time questions via Slack or Teams. For US clients we adjust start times to create an afternoon or early evening overlap window.
How long does the hiring process take?+
From signed agreement and onboarding fee to your bookkeeper's first day is typically three to four weeks. Week one we shortlist. Week two you conduct candidate video calls. Week three we finalise the employment contract, NDA, and technical setup. Week four your bookkeeper starts. Compare that to the four-to-five month average for a local AU hire.
Can I speak directly with my bookkeeper?+
Yes — and we encourage it. Your bookkeeper is not hidden behind a helpdesk. You will have their direct Slack or Teams handle and can ask questions, share files, and give direction just as you would with an in-office team member. SARCS Group sits in the background managing performance and quality — we're not a barrier between you and your staff member.
How do you handle Nepal public holidays?+
Nepal has more public holidays than Australia or the USA. We notify you in advance of Nepal public holidays and agree a plan for any time-sensitive work. Staff do not work on Nepal public holidays without advance agreement and additional compensation — this is clearly laid out in the service agreement so there are no surprises.
What currencies do you invoice in?+
We invoice in USD as standard. Australian clients can request AUD invoicing — the rate is fixed at the exchange rate on the invoice date. Payment is via international wire transfer (SWIFT). Bank transfer fees are on the client's side. We issue invoices on the 1st of each month, due within 14 days.
Can we eventually hire the staff member directly?+
Our service agreement includes a 12-month non-solicitation clause from the end of the engagement. If you wish to directly employ a staff member, we offer a structured buyout arrangement — typically USD $4,000–6,000 covering our recruitment and training investment. This can be discussed on your discovery call if it's a consideration for you.
